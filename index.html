How to install Ford Bronco grab handles

Every grab handle on this list installs the same basic way — no drilling, no special tools. Here is the standard process:

  1. Open the Bronco door and locate the roll bar bolt points — there are two per side, front and rear.
  2. Remove the existing plastic bolt covers if present (pop off by hand).
  3. Thread the grab handle loop through the bolt point or wrap around the roll bar as directed.
  4. Feed the mounting strap or hardware through the loop and tighten by hand.
  5. Tug firmly to confirm it seats tight. No torque wrench required.
  6. Repeat for all four positions (front driver, front passenger, rear driver, rear passenger).

Total install time: 10–15 minutes for a full 4-pack. Bartact handles ship with written instructions. Amazon handles typically include a diagram card. If your Bronco has the Sport trim, check that your roll bar bolt placement matches the listed fitment before ordering.

2-door vs 4-door Ford Bronco grab handles: is there a difference?

The short answer: most grab handles on this list fit both 2-door and 4-door Broncos because they wrap the same roll bar bolt points used on both configurations. However, there are a few things to know:

  • 4-pack vs 2-pack: The 4-door Bronco has rear passengers who need handles too. Always buy a 4-pack. The 2-door only needs a front pair, so a 2-pack works — but most owners buy 4-packs anyway for coverage.
  • Roll bar position: The 2-door Bronco roll bar sits closer to the front seats. The 4-door has both front and rear roll bar sections. All handles on this list are designed for the shared bolt pattern.
  • Bartact fitment: Bartact sells separate listings for 2-door and 4-door configurations. Make sure you select the correct one at checkout.
  • Amazon listings: Most say “2021-2025 2-door and 4-door” — this is accurate for the standard roll bar configuration.

Bottom line: if your listing says it fits both 2-door and 4-door 2021–2025 Broncos, you are safe. If it only says 2-door or only says 4-door, buy accordingly.

What to look for when buying Ford Bronco grab handles

1. Custom-fit vs universal: Bartact makes custom-cut handles designed specifically for the Bronco roll bar diameter and bolt spacing. Amazon handles are universal-fit — they work, but the wrap may not be as tight or clean. If exact fit matters to you, go Bartact.

2. Paracord rating: Real 550 paracord holds 550 lbs of breaking strength. Most Amazon listings claim 550 paracord — YANWEN and SEVEN SPARTA have been verified. Knockoffs may use thinner cord that looks the same but doesn’t hold the same load.

3. Number of handles: A 4-pack covers all four roll bar positions on a 4-door Bronco. A 2-pack covers the front pair. For doorless trail use with rear passengers, always get a 4-pack.

4. Hardware quality: Check that the mounting buckle or strap hardware is stainless or coated steel, not bare aluminum that will pit and corrode after a few seasons. All picks on this list meet this standard.

5. Color and style: Paracord handles come in dozens of color combinations. Bartact offers the widest range. Most Amazon picks ship in a few standard colors. If color-matching your interior is important, Bartact gives you the most options.
