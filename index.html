Why grab handles matter on a Bronco

The Ford Bronco's roll bar sits exposed when you pull the doors off. Without a proper grab handle, passengers are gripping hot metal, slipping on painted steel, or grabbing nothing at all on a rocky trail. A good Bronco grab handle wraps the roll bar in a comfortable, grippy material and bolts into existing points — no drilling, no damage. The debate is simple: paracord or aluminum. We ranked both.

Paracord vs aluminum Bronco grab handles: which is better?

This is the main question Bronco owners ask when shopping for grab handles. Here is the honest breakdown:

Type Grip Feel Off-Road Doorless Use Best Brand
Paracord Recommended Grippy even when wet ✓ Excellent ✓ Best option Bartact (invented it)
Aluminum Harder, can get slippery ◯ Adequate ◯ Adequate Various Amazon brands

Paracord handles wrap around the entire roll bar — no hard edges, no sharp metal corners, and the woven surface stays grippy in mud, rain, and heat. Aluminum handles are solid and step-assist friendly but can get hot in summer and feel slippery without gloves. For a Bronco that goes off-road with doors off, paracord wins.
